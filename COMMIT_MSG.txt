Batch runs from a CSV, lysine surface attachment, per-run box height, run timing

Planning and submitting
- analyze.ipynb: the setup section now plans a whole CSV of runs at once
  (one row per simulation, blank cells fall back to a Shared block), prints
  a comparison table + per-run stats and lattice plots, checks every name
  against simulations/ and used-runs.csv before anything is queued, submits
  them all, and can smoke-test one row locally on CPU/CUDA.
- tools/run_batch.py (new): CSV -> RunSpec/RunPlan, ELP shorthand or raw
  sequences, per-row walltime/mode/crosslinking, the used-runs.csv registry
  (check_names / record_runs / backfill) and the summary printers.
- tools/cli.py: `sim used` (--backfill) shows the registry; `sim submit`
  records to it; `sim crosslink` handles free/preattached runs.
- Example-csv.csv, used-runs.csv (backfilled from simulations/),
  block-runs.csv: monoblock / triblock-32 / triblock-64 / tetrablock in
  brush+XL, free +/- XL and preattached +/- XL (20 runs, submitted as SLURM jobs 265624-265643 with
  reaction/surface start step 1,000,000).

Attaching chains through lysines (modes free / preattached)
- tools/surface.py (new): chains hang from lysines tethered to a plane
  above the wall instead of a fixed residue-0 anchor. `free` starts with one
  random lysine per chain bonded and binds more during the run; `preattached`
  pins a random fraction at t=0. Hairpin-stack initial construction with a
  geometry check, energy report before/after minimisation, per-run seed.
- tools/crosslink.py: shares the site list and event record with the
  surface binder (kind = surface rows, new `origin` column), masks
  surface-bonded lysines out of the K-K pair pool, drop_anchor only in brush
  mode, post-hoc detection replays surface bonds.
- tools/new_simulation.py, template/run.py: --mode and the surface_*
  settings; surface.yaml is the opt-in, so brush mode is the same code path
  as before.

Box height (was too small)
- The box is periodic in z and the wall is not a lid: beads within the 4 nm
  cutoff of the top feel the periodic image of the grafted layer. The
  720-1040-residue blocks reached 51-63 nm in the fixed 50 nm box.
- plan_box_height: default 0.3 x contour length (rounded up to 5 nm, min
  50 nm); `box_height` in Shared / `--box-height` overrides. BoxPlan carries
  l_box_z; the notebook load cell prints the headroom and warns.
- tools/metadata.py records z_min_nm, z_max_nm, box_headroom_nm,
  box_tall_enough after each run.

Run timing and metadata
- template/run.py times sim.simulate() and writes timing.yaml (legs add up
  across checkpoint restarts); metadata.csv gains run_wall_seconds,
  ns_per_hour, walltime_requested and the surface-bond statistics.

Docs and notes

- notes_titus: why x/y wrapping is bookkeeping not physics, why minimum-image
